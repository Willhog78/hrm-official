# Own experienced procedural continuity — stage 4

The owner authorized continuation from draft PR #43. The pre-edit assessment
identified recipe injection, cyclic reward inflation, stale links, bodily-state
drift breaking procedures, and execution that could bypass urgent needs or real
materials. Changes are restricted to transition memory/evaluation, version routing
in interaction/thermal learning, configuration and experimental arm selection.
Physical primitives, action costs, quantities, physiology, deployment settings,
ports and environment handling are unchanged.

## Explicit experimental mode

`experienced-transitions-v4` extends V3's food and delayed thermal attribution.
`none`, V1, V2 and V3 remain available with their existing behavior. V4 requires
capacities and size-scaled received-energy accounting. The qualification arm is
`v1-transitionsv4`; no production runner was switched. Checkpoint fingerprints
reject implicit conversion to an earlier version.

## Learning continuity

V4 keeps the 64-outcome transition model and adds at most 128 links between actual
own attempts. A link requires consecutive recorded actions in the same tick or
adjacent ticks, with the previously experienced after-state matching the next
actual before-state. It is created only if the first action newly enabled the
second, or both actions changed an overlapping perceptible material aspect.
Aspect overlap distinguishes held/ground/worn object classes, individual pool
indices and geometry. Body energy, hydration and fatigue do not count as material
progress. A nearby unrelated heavy-stone pickup cannot link to a cut that changes
only accessible tissue. Links need two observed occurrences before planning uses
them. They expire after 96 ticks without reexperience and are removed when either
endpoint is forgotten or evicted.

This is bounded individual adjacency memory, not an action script. No full recipe
array, peer reward, central library, inherited procedure or semantic construction
goal is supplied. Material-aspect overlap is a conservative dependence cue, not a
complete causal proof. Coarse forms can alias different objects; same-class
correlations remain an explicit limitation.

## Matching and evaluation

V4 matching ignores energy, hydration and fatigue bands so normal overnight bodily
changes do not sever an intact material procedure. Injury, weather, skin wetness,
position, physical object/pool forms, geometry, affordances and current access
remain distinct. Access is not discarded: a redundant cut cannot borrow the value
of its first successful counterpart. Current reserves still guard live selection;
the existing fatigue and physical capability guards still control execution.

Evaluation looks up to six actions ahead with the existing 128-expansion bound.
Repeating an action is allowed only along observed links through different states.
A branch cannot revisit a previously searched state. No-op loops and closed cycles
cannot repeatedly collect one terminal reward. Two unknown adverse outcomes shrink
each action's expected benefit; all observed failures and actual costs still count.
Continuation is discounted by the existing 0.9 factor. Alternative failures remain
in an action's expectation even if only successful outcomes have useful links.

Every chosen step is rescored against current local options. There is no committed
macro, reservation or predicted state applied to the world. The three physical
interactions per tick limit is unchanged. A procedure can resume next tick only if
its current perceptible state, learned value, physical options and reserves support
the next action. Leaving, changed material or urgent needs may prevent resumption.

## Evidence and limits

The real controlled stone fixture begins with a blunt basaltic stone, a blunt
fine-grained core and known fresh tissue. Forty explicitly controlled preparation
opportunities produce 21 sharp-fragment/cutting successes and 19 unpaid blunt
outcomes. Fracture, mass conservation, costs, basal energy, physiology, food receipt
and refusal use existing functions. The four-action path is grasp blunt stone,
strike core, grasp resulting edge, then cut on the next tick. The terminal meal
alone supplies value. Initial grasp value is -0.0035 basal units at depth three
and approximately +0.30535 at the six-step horizon.

After that training, the unpatched live interaction loop selects the first three
physical steps at tick 83, and the chooser selects cutting at tick 84 after the
basal-energy and physiology update. At tick 81, a real blunt fracture stops after
two actions; the remembered sharp result is not materialized. Learned memory also
round-trips through JSON without changing valuation. Training actions and materials
are controlled; this demonstrates learned execution across ticks, not autonomous
stone-tool discovery in the ecology.

Ten new micro tests cover six-step depth, repeatable progress, observed adjacency,
single/nonconsecutive experience, current weather/access/injury, unrelated preceding
acts, cycles, bounded/aged links, urgent/missing-material guards and the real
four-action example. Two new integration tests verify exact weather checkpoint
replay, bounds and explicit mode/accounting requirements.

235 distinct tests passed: 174 micro and 61 selected integration/regression tests.
Default fast qualification and V4 smoke each passed three seeds × 75 days with no
failures or warnings. Observed/unobserved ledger digests match; maximum relative
element error is approximately 1.21e-15, and the three-interaction limit holds.
Normal three-seed, 75-day V4 worlds produce zero value-driven sequence choices.
Full-repository tests and long-run survival qualification were not run.
Machine-readable evidence: `experiments/genesis/summaries/PROCEDURAL_CONTINUITY_V4_2026-10-09.json`.

The small wood arrangement remains physically uneconomical. Six actions are still
a bounded horizon, not a complete solution to material accumulation, arbitrary
long procedures, navigation between work sites or environmental interruptions.
No shelter invention, sequential imitation or cumulative cultural transmission is
demonstrated. The next evidence gap is independent discovery under measured real
opportunities, before transferring procedures to observers.
