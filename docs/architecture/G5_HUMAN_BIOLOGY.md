# G5 — Human Biology

## Purpose

G5 introduces humans as biological organisms inside the already-qualified autonomous ecology.

Humans do not receive a separate substrate. They must obey the same world, matter, water, producer, and consumer authorities established by G0–G4.

## In scope

- human body mass and composition
- body water
- tracked elemental inventory
- metabolism and energy use
- feeding and drinking through existing ecological resources
- hunger and thirst consequences
- movement costs
- growth and aging
- injury and biological death
- reproduction and offspring material transfer
- decomposition / return of body matter after death
- deterministic checkpoint and replay behavior
- conservation and ledger qualification

## Explicitly out of scope

G5 must not introduce:

- cognition
- memory
- language
- planning
- beliefs or preferences
- tools or technology
- professions
- families as social institutions
- culture
- government
- civilization
- scripted survival assistance
- target populations
- population floors
- observer feedback into the simulation

Those belong to later gates.

## Authority boundary

The human layer may request or consume resources through existing interfaces, but it may not own terrain, climate, elemental pools, producer state, or non-human consumer state.

Human biology owns only human organism state and human biological transitions.

## Core invariant

Nothing comes from nowhere; nothing disappears without a sink.

Every human birth, growth event, meal, drink, waste/death transfer, and decomposition path must be materially accounted for through the existing matter ledger.

## Initial implementation target

The first G5 qualification should prove that a deterministic human organism can:

1. exist as materially accounted biological state;
2. consume food and water from the existing world;
3. incur metabolic and movement costs;
4. age and die from biological causes;
5. return material to the ecological system after death;
6. reproduce without creating matter;
7. replay identically from checkpoint;
8. preserve element and water accounting.

This is a biology gate, not an intelligence gate.
