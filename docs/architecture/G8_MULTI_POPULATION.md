# G8 — Multi-Population World

## Goal

Run geographically separated human populations with independent local histories and no scripted social outcome.

## Geography

The world is divided into four initial founder regions. Region membership records origin only; it does not encode culture, ideology, profession, technology, or destiny.

Each founder pair is materially seeded from biomass and water available inside its own region.

## Travel

Humans retain the ordinary one-cell movement and local perception rules. No hard wall prevents crossing a region boundary. Separation therefore constrains contact by distance rather than making contact impossible.

## History

Each population learns only from what its members locally perceive and experience. Their cognition, memory, expectations, and learned primitive action sequences remain individual state.

## Explicitly deferred

- nations;
- tribes as political institutions;
- governments;
- economies;
- religions;
- scripted conflict;
- scripted trade;
- authored cultural traits;
- technology trees;
- historical milestones.

## Exit condition

At least four founder populations begin in distinct regions under measurably different local ecological conditions. After independent experience, their learned location preferences or resource strategies differ. Travel remains local and cross-region contact remains possible but is not forced.
