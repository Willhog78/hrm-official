# G10.2A — Survival Affordance Substrate

## Purpose

Give calibrated humans physically available ways to reduce environmental exposure before any long-run human survival experiment.

This layer adds possibilities to the world. It does not add named survival technologies or scripted survival behavior.

## Causal rules

- Woody biomass is distinct from edible plant biomass.
- Woody structure develops only after sustained viable plant growth.
- Exposed rock is derived from elevation and local terrain relief.
- Cave/overhang-like natural cover exists only where high rock exposure and strong relief coincide.
- Human thermal exposure is moderated by occupying cells with real canopy or terrain cover.
- Humans may perceive local woody material, rock exposure, and natural cover.
- No build-shelter, make-fire, make-clothing, or equivalent named action is introduced.

## Non-goals

This slice does not yet model:
- individual tree objects or named tree species;
- detailed geology or karst chemistry;
- fire;
- clothing;
- constructed shelters;
- scripted technology discovery.

## Qualification intent

Before the long-run calibrated-human experiment, the substrate must demonstrate:

1. natural cover is a deterministic consequence of terrain conditions;
2. woody material does not appear under impossible growth conditions;
3. woody material is not automatically edible;
4. occupying physical cover measurably changes exposure cost;
5. existing coordination, conservation, and replay behavior remains intact.
