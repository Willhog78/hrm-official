# G7 — General Action and Communication

## Goal

Allow useful behavior to be composed, learned, signaled, and imitated from general primitives.

## Primitive vocabulary

move, inspect, grasp, release, carry, consume, transfer, combine, separate, apply_force, arrange, signal.

These are causal verbs, not technologies or milestones.

## Rule

The causal engine may store primitive sequences and their experienced value. It must not contain named techniques such as farming, spear-making, shelter-building, cooking, professions, or civilization stages.

## Communication

An agent may signal a bounded primitive sequence to a nearby recognized agent. The receiver may retain and imitate it. Transmission does not guarantee usefulness; reward must come from executing the sequence in the environment.

## Exit condition

At least one useful primitive sequence must be acquired by one agent and transmitted to another agent, which then succeeds by imitation, while no named technology exists in causal state.
