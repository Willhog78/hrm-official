# Work continuity: return alone is not the missing mechanism

Three untrained 365-day worlds using the unchanged V4, wind/subcell, local-work and incremental-surface bundle were observed. Every config fingerprint and ledger digest exactly matches the captured PR #48 world. Chain integrity and element/water/lithic conservation pass. The census supplies no choices, instructions, material or reward to agents.

## Own wood work persists across visits

| Seed | Own actor/cell worksites | Sites worked on multiple days | Sites returned to | Departures | Observed returns |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| demography-a | 13 | 4 | 5 | 53 | 44 |
| demography-b | 11 | 7 | 9 | 40 | 32 |
| g10-4-01 | 14 | 6 | 9 | 41 | 31 |
| Total | 38 | 17 | 23 | 134 | 107 |

A site is registered only by that actor's actual physical wood progress. The total includes 67 progress actions and 2,320 interaction agent-days at previously worked cells. These are previously worked places, not owned projects or individually attributable retained wood stocks. Returns are counted at a subsequent movement decision; terminal arrivals or deaths before that decision can be missed.

Of 134 departures, 68 followed a last interaction observation of loose wood. This stock observation may be stale. Locally adequate food was perceived on 40 departures and adequate water on all 134. These are co-occurrences, not proof of why an agent left. All 134 had location memory, but none contained loose/arranged material fields. The existing food/water memory can nevertheless lead back to a previously worked place.

## Availability usually does not become repeated work

| Interaction | Offered agent-days | Reserve-ready offered agent-days | Untried offered agent-days | Nonpositive learned value offered agent-days | Choice decisions with option | Actual selections |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Force wood | 6,591 | 6,586 | 821 | 5,770 | 7,231 | 39 |
| Arrange loose wood | 1,314 | 1,313 | 411 | 903 | 1,389 | 28 |
| Extend held surface | 45 | 45 | 45 | 0 | 46 | 1 |

No positive single-action learned value was observed for these keys when offered. Value labels describe actual cognition, not hypothetical sequence forecasts. Actor-day labels may overlap when value or choice changes between physical steps; choice-decision counts are a separate denominator. The existing reserve guard was satisfied almost everywhere these options occurred, so it is not the main exclusion in these sampled early worlds.

For arranging wood, 1,283 offered agent-days included at least one choice returning no action; 73 included another selected action. These overlap and are not a partition of the 1,314 offered days. For extension, 42 of the 45 days included no selected action and three included another action. The one extension was untried at the time of selection. This census does not identify the reason for every non-selection or establish a causal treatment effect.

## Removed surfaces are not invariably lost

There were 20 actual own worn-surface removal events and 10 subsequent successful reacquisition events by the same remover, nine on a later day. Repeated events can involve one object, so these are not percentages of unique surfaces. Failed grasps and other actors' grasps do not count. Reacquisition and revisiting demonstrate continuity mechanisms, not improvement in protection or learned useful sequences.

## Development implication

A material-location field is missing, but a blanket return-to-work rule would address only part of the observed boundary and could encode a construction objective. The stronger measured gap is converting physically available opportunities into sufficiently repeated, consequential experiments. Existing single-action values are nonpositive; PR #48 already established negligible thermal benefit and no sequence-value choices. There is no evidence here that remembering a place would make those actions economically useful.

The next targeted work should examine general costed exploration and recognition of previously experienced enabling transitions under changing local conditions. Validate recognition, experimental repetition, and eventual real physiological payoff separately. Any change must retain biological priorities, physical action limits and actual material access; neither remembered work nor geometric progress may be assigned invented physiological benefit. Cultural persistence and improvement remain unproven.
