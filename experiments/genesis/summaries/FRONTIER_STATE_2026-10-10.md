## Next boundary

Before changing trial probability or abstracting more state, measure the exact exclusion points: absent matching physical experience, changed environmental/material prerequisites, lack of an enabled successor, exhausted attempts, and trial draw/earlier choice. Those are distinct from ordinary reserve/fatigue drift. Recognition of relevant prerequisites must stay grounded in what the agent perceived; neither geometric progress nor observer expectations may become invented physiological reward. Useful protective practice, imitation of sequences and cultural persistence remain unproven.
